01 / PERSONAL REFLECTION

Let your actions be your light.

A short reflection on faith, honesty, and being responsible online.

“

My Reflection

For me, putting God first means trying to do what is right every day. I can show my faith by being honest, respecting others, and helping when I can. Online, I should think before I post and avoid sharing hurtful or false messages. Even small good actions can make a difference.

“Let your light shine before others.” — Matthew 5:16

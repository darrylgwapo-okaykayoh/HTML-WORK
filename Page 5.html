05 / FAITH AND TECHNOLOGY

Use your screen to spread good.

Technology can be more than entertainment. We can use it to share hope and support others.

✦

Share hope

Post a kind message or an inspiring Bible verse.

⌁

Learn and teach

Use websites and videos to learn new things and help others learn.

♡

Stay respectful

Talk to people with care, even when you have different views.

“Use your gifts to make the world a little brighter.”

One kind choice at a time.

MY MESSAGE AS A STUDENT

Good values matter online and offline.

Technology is part of our daily life. We can use it to learn, share true information, encourage others, and show care. Putting God first means choosing what is right, even when no one is watching.

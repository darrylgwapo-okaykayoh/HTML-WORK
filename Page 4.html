04 / EDUCATIONAL PAGE

Think before
you click.

Make the internet a safer and kinder place with these simple habits.

1.

Be kind. Do not bully or shame people online.

2.

Check first. Make sure information is true before sharing it.

3.

Keep private things private. Do not share passwords or personal details.

4.

Give credit. Name the creator when you use their work.
